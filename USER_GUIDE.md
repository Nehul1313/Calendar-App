# Calendar App User Guide

Welcome to the Calendar App! This guide will help you understand all the features available to you and how to navigate the application.

## 1. Getting Started

- **Login/Register:** To get started, log in with your credentials or create a new account.
- **Default Calendar:** Upon registration, a new calendar is automatically created for you, named exactly after your username.
- **Home Navigation:** You can always click the **CME Calendar** logo in the top left corner of the navigation bar to return to your main calendar dashboard.

## 2. Managing Your Calendars

You can create multiple calendars to organize different areas of your life (e.g., Work, Personal, Hobbies).

- **Create a Calendar:** In the left sidebar under "My Calendars", click the small `+` icon, type a name, and press **Create**.
- **Rename a Calendar:** Hover over one of your calendars in the sidebar and click the pencil `✎` icon.
- **Delete a Calendar:** Hover over a calendar and click the trash `🗑` icon. (Note: You cannot delete your default calendar).

## 3. Creating and Managing Events

- **Create an Event:** Simply click on any day (in month view) or drag over a time slot (in week/day view) to open the event creation menu. You can specify:
  - Title
  - Start and End Date/Time (or select "All day")
  - Recurring Rules (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly)
  - Location
  - Description
  - Color Label
- **Edit an Event:** Click on an existing event to edit its details.
- **Move an Event:** Drag and drop events directly on the calendar grid to change their dates or times.
- **Delete an Event:** Click on an event and use the "Delete" button in the dialog. For recurring events, you will be prompted to choose whether to delete just **This event**, **This and following events**, or **All events** in the series.

## 4. Public vs Private Calendars

You can control whether other people can see your calendars. By default, newly created calendars are **Public**.

- **Public:** Other users can search for this calendar, preview its events, and subscribe to it.
- **Private:** Only you can see the calendar and its events.
- **How to change visibility:** 
  1. Click your Profile icon in the top right corner.
  2. Select **Settings**.
  3. You will see a list of all your calendars. Click **Make Private** or **Make Public** to toggle their visibility.

## 5. Browsing and Subscribing to Calendars

You can discover public calendars created by other users and add them to your dashboard.

- **Browse Calendars:** Click the **Browse Calendars** button in the top navigation bar.
- **Search:** Use the search bar to find calendars by their name or the owner's username.
- **Preview:** Click on a calendar's name in the search results to see a quick preview of its events without subscribing to it.
- **Subscribe:** If you want to keep track of a calendar, click the **Subscribe** button. 
- **Other Calendars:** Once subscribed, the calendar will appear in your left sidebar under the "Other Calendars" section. You can view its events alongside your own.
- **Unsubscribe:** You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the `X` next to the calendar in your sidebar, or from the Browse page.

## 6. Importing and Exporting (ICS)

The app fully supports `.ics` files, meaning you can integrate with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook.

- **Export:** Select a calendar in your sidebar, then click the **Export** button in the top navigation bar. This will download an `.ics` file containing all your events.
- **Import:** Select a calendar in your sidebar, then click the **Import** button. Choose an `.ics` file from your computer, and the events will be added to your selected calendar.

## 7. Profile and Security

- **Secure URLs:** Your calendar views are securely protected. The app uses long, unguessable UUIDs in the web address so nobody can accidentally stumble upon your private calendar.
- **Change Password:** Click your Profile icon and select **Reset Password** to change your login credentials.
- **Logout:** Click your Profile icon and select **Logout** to securely end your session.
